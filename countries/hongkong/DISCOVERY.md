## Framing

Hong Kong is a Special Administrative Region of the People's Republic of
China, not a sovereign state. The atlas compares it as an **economy** — its
own currency board, its own statistical system, its own customs territory —
and never presents SAR statistics as Chinese national ones (nor the reverse).
