# Migration

The Census Population Estimates Program publishes net international migration
but explicitly does not publish its gross components separately. The gross-flow
slot therefore remains pending; DHS admissions, visas, removals and encounters
are incompatible legal or enforcement events and are not substituted. The net
line is the committed one-vintage PEP rate. Stock and the citizenship lens use
the ACS B05002 universe and denominator; 2020 remains missing because the
standard ACS 1-year estimates were not released.
